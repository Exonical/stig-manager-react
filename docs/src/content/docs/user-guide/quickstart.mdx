This walks through one full evaluation loop: log in, create a Collection,
attach STIGs to an Asset, complete some Reviews, and export a CKL / POA&M.

If your organisation already has Collections you've been added to, you can
skip the Collection creation step.

## Concepts in 30 seconds

- **Collection** — top-level container. Mirrors an RMF Package, a Lab, or
  whatever grouping makes sense for you.
- **Asset** — anything you evaluate (server, image, network device). Belongs
  to one Collection.
- **STIG** — the canonical DISA checklist (a Benchmark + its Revisions).
- **Review** — one per `(Asset, Rule)`. Carries result, detail, comment,
  status, and history.
- **Grant** — `(User-or-Group, Collection, Role)`. Controls visibility and
  edit rights.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. **Log in.**

   You'll be redirected to your organisation's identity provider (Keycloak,
   Okta, Entra ID, etc.). Authenticate with whatever your org uses (SSO,
   CAC, password + MFA). On success you'll be returned to the STIG Manager
   home page.

2. **Look at the navigation rail.**

   The left rail lists every Collection you have access to, plus the
   global **STIG Library** and (for users with the privilege) **Admin**.
   Collections you own are flagged with a small badge.

3. **Create your first Collection.**

   Click **+ New Collection** in the rail. Give it a name and an optional
   description. You'll be made Owner of the new Collection and dropped into
   its Configuration tab.

4. **Add some Assets.**

   From Configuration → Assets → **New Asset**, enter at least a name. You
   can also paste a CSV or upload a `.ckl` / `.cklb` /
   `.xccdf` file to create Assets in bulk — see
   [Imports](/user-guide/concepts#imports).

5. **Attach STIGs to Assets.**

   On an Asset, open **STIG Attachments** and pick from the Library. The
   Library tracks every Revision of every STIG you've imported. The latest
   Revision is selected by default.

6. **Optionally grant access to colleagues.**

   Configuration → Grants → **New Grant**. Pick a user or group, pick a
   role (Restricted / Full / Manage / Owner). For Restricted, define ACL
   entries pointing at specific Assets, STIGs, or Labels.

7. **Evaluate.**

   From the Collection home, open the **Review** workspace. Pick an
   `(Asset, STIG)` pair to see every Rule. For each Rule:
   - Read Check, Fix, CCI, and Control mappings in the right inspector.
   - Set the Result (`Open`, `Not a Finding`, `Not Applicable`,
     `Informational`, `Not Reviewed`).
   - Add Detail and (if required by the Collection) a Comment.
   - Save. The status moves to `In Progress` (or whatever workflow your
     Collection requires).

8. **Submit for review.**

   Once you've evaluated everything assigned to you, click **Submit
   Reviews** on the workspace toolbar. Reviewers with Manage or Owner can
   Accept (lock) or Reject (return to evaluator) individual Reviews.

9. **Export.**

   From the Collection toolbar:
   - **Export CKLs** → zip of one `.ckl` per `(Asset, STIG)`.
   - **Export CKLBs** → same but JSON.
   - **Export XCCDF Results** → XCCDF v1.2 results files.
   - **Export POA&M** → eMASS-compatible xlsx (default or MCCAST template).

</Steps>

<Aside>
  All of the above is also doable from the API. See the
  [API reference](/reference/api) and the
  [authentication notes](/installation/authentication) for scopes.
</Aside>
